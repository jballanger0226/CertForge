How do I make a CSR?

Most servers, routers, NAS boxes and hypervisors have a "generate CSR" button in their certificate settings. On a Linux machine with OpenSSL:

openssl req -new -newkey ec -pkeyopt ec_paramgen_curve:P-256 -nodes \
  -keyout server.key -out server.csr \
  -subj "/CN=nas.home.arpa" -addext "subjectAltName=DNS:nas.home.arpa,IP:192.168.1.20"

Keep server.key on the server. Only server.csr comes here.
